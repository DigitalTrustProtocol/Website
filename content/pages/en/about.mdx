The Digital Trust Protocol is an open project to let people decide for themselves whose judgment guides their attention online. It builds on Nostr, so trust statements and ratings are signed by their authors and are not owned by any platform, including ours.

## Contact

Email [attention@trust.dance](mailto:attention@trust.dance). For bugs and proposals, open an issue in the relevant [repository](/code).

## Team

The project is maintained by Carsten Keutmann and contributors. Contributions are welcome in every repository.

## This site and your privacy

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, trackers, or third-party scripts. Fonts and scripts are served from this site only, and a strict Content Security Policy blocks everything else. If you choose a light or dark theme, that choice is kept in your browser's local storage and never sent anywhere.
