## What it does on X

- **Trust people.** Mark an account as trusted, Neutral, or distrusted. On any profile you see whether your circle trusts them and how that trust reaches you.
- **Rate posts.** One tap gives a post a rating, from *worth reading* down to *spam*, with optional labels and a short note.
- **See your circle's judgment in place.** A small indicator next to names and posts shows the evidence from people you trust; the details and the path are a click away.
- **Hide what misses your bar.** Optional filters keep posts from distrusted or unrated authors out of your home timeline. Filters are off until you turn them on.

## Privacy in short

- Attention reads public information that X already shows on the page: numeric account ids, handles, and post ids. From the public `twid` cookie it keeps only your own numeric X id.
- It never posts, likes, follows, deletes, or sends messages for you, and it does not search X.
- Your Nostr key lives in an encrypted vault inside the extension and never reaches the page or any server.
- There is no Attention server and no analytics. Data leaves your device only to the Nostr relays you choose and, for a few settings, Chrome Sync.

The [full privacy policy](/attention/privacy) lists every item.

## Install

The Chrome Web Store listing is being prepared. Until it is public, you can build Attention from source:

1. Install Node.js 22 or newer, clone [BrowserExtension](https://github.com/DigitalTrustProtocol/BrowserExtension), and run `npm install` and `npm run check`.
2. Open `chrome://extensions`, turn on **Developer mode**, choose **Load unpacked**, and select the `dist` folder.
3. Open `x.com` and follow the onboarding in the Attention side panel to create or import a key.

## Try it with sample data

Demo mode fills Attention with a sample web of trust, so you can try every feature before you publish anything. Ratings and trust you give in demo mode stay on your device.
