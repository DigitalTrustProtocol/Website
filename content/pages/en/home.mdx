<Section title="Two questions, kept apart" id="two-questions">

A follow is not trust, and a like is not a rating. The protocol asks two separate questions, because mixing them turns crowd gestures into fake trust.

<CardGrid columns={2}>
<Question kind="32009" question="Is this identity worth my trust?">

A **trust statement**: trust, Neutral, or distrust about a person or account. Only trust lets your circle grow; it decides whose judgments you read.

</Question>
<Question kind="32014" question="Is this artifact worth my time?">

A **rating** from 0 to 100 for a post, page, or product. Ratings are advice from people you already trust; they never widen your circle.

</Question>
</CardGrid>

<Section title="Principles" id="principles">

<Principles>
<Principle title="Your judgments are yours">Signed with your key and published to Nostr relays, so they move with you between apps.</Principle>
<Principle title="Answers are relative to you">Two people can get different answers about the same account, and both are honest.</Principle>
<Principle title="No central scorer">Evidence comes from people you trust, with the path that led to it. Nobody ranks the world.</Principle>
<Principle title="Servers are optional and verifiable">A server saves work; it is not an oracle. Every answer cites signed events anyone can check.</Principle>
</Principles>
